This guide explains the Classic Maps server ranking. The Dodgeball server has its own ranking with different scoring (see [What Is TF2 Dodgeball](/en/blog/what-is-tf2-dodgeball/)).

The server ranking runs on HLstatsZ: every kill and every match objective (such as capturing points or carrying the intel) adds points to your score. You can follow it from the in-game chat, using the `rank`, `top10`, `session` and `place` commands, or on the ranking website.

## How points are calculated

Kills and objectives raise your score. The exact calculation is done by HLstatsZ and may take factors such as your opponent's performance into account, so the same kill isn't always worth the same number of points. For details, the ranking site shows each player's history and stats.

## Minimum players to score

So that nearly empty matches don't distort the ranking, the server only counts points when there is a minimum number of players in the match. That minimum is set by the staff and may be adjusted; if in doubt, ask an admin on Discord.

## In-game chat commands

Type in the in-game chat:

- `rank`: shows your score and position.
- `top10`: lists the 10 best players.
- `session`: shows your performance in the current session.
- `place`: shows your overall place in the ranking.

## Where to see the full ranking

The full ranking, with stats per player, weapon and map, is at [stats.skynet-tf2.com.br](https://stats.skynet-tf2.com.br/hlstats.php?game=tf).
