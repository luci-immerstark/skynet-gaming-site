Zombie Infection is Team Fortress 2's official zombie mode: everyone starts as a survivor on the RED team, a few players become zombies on BLU, and whoever dies becomes a zombie too. The zombies win if they infect everyone, and the survivors win if they last until time runs out. We already have a Zombie Infection server online: see how to join at the end of this post.

## What is Zombie Infection

Every match starts with everyone on RED and a setup period. When it ends, a few players are picked to become zombies: from 1 to 5, depending on the number of players. Whoever dies on RED becomes a zombie on BLU, so the horde only grows. Each infection adds 8 seconds to the clock, and RED wins if any survivor lasts until time runs out.

To keep matches balanced, when 3 survivors are left they deal mini-crits, and the last survivor deals crits.

## How it started

The facts below come from the [Team Fortress Wiki](https://wiki.teamfortress.com/wiki/Zombie_Infection):

- The mode was made by the community in VScript and officially added by Valve during the Scream Fortress XV Halloween event, in October 2023.
- Before it, TF2 zombie modes existed as community server plugins, such as Zombie Fortress.
- In October 2024 the mode got the `zi_blazehattan` map, and the teams were renamed Survivors (RED) and Zombies (BLU).

## How to play

![RED Engineer with a wrench facing a zombie Heavy](../../../assets/img/zombie-infection-engineer.png)
*Surviving takes teamwork.*

**Tips for survivors**

- **Stay close to your team.** Whoever wanders alone becomes the first infection.
- **Use buildings.** Sentries deal 60% less damage to zombies, and the Heavy's minigun deals 40% less, so don't rely on them alone.
- **Pick your weapon well.** The Pomson 6000 puts the zombie's ability on cooldown for 3 seconds, and the Homewrecker destroys the Spy's EMP grenade.
- **Watch out for the Spy.** A survivor Spy can also disguise as a zombie.

**Tips for zombies**

- **Use your ability.** Every class has one, and the cooldowns are short: see the list right below.
- **Attack as a group.** The zombie Medic heals allies, and the zombie Engineer creates a respawn point close to the action.
- **Every infection counts.** It adds 8 seconds to the clock and puts one more zombie in the match.

## Zombie abilities

![Grid with the faces of the 9 zombified classes, eyes glowing orange](../../../assets/img/zombie-infection-classes.jpg)
*All 9 classes turn into zombies, each with its own ability.*

- **Scout, Speed Demon:** passive. 75% faster, 25% higher jump, triple jump and 75 health.
- **Soldier, Pounce:** leaps forward, and landing on someone's head is a stomp. 5 s cooldown.
- **Pyro, Sticky Spew:** spits tar that hampers survivors' mobility weapons. Immune to liquids and explodes on death. 10 s cooldown.
- **Demoman, Blast Charge:** a 275-damage explosion around him. 6 s cooldown.
- **Heavy, Rock Hurl:** hurls concrete that stuns on a direct hit, knocks back and disables buildings. 10 s cooldown.
- **Engineer, Beacon:** creates a respawn point for the zombies. 15 s cooldown.
- **Medic, Heal:** heals and protects nearby allies from damage, but can't attack while using it. 7 s cooldown.
- **Sniper, Spit:** an acid ball that damages survivors and buildings, and leaves an acid pool on death. 10 s cooldown.
- **Spy, EMP Grenade:** disables buildings and reveals survivors, and is passively invisible. 10 s cooldown.
