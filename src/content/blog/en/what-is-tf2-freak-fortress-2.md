Freak Fortress 2, or just FF2, is Team Fortress 2's "one against all" mode: each round, one player becomes a giant boss with lots of health and special abilities, and the rest of the server teams up to take them down. The boss wins by eliminating everyone; the team wins by draining the boss's health. SKYNET now has a Freak Fortress 2 server: see how to join at the end of the post.

## What is Freak Fortress 2

FF2 runs on **arena** maps, where nobody respawns during the round. One player is picked to be the boss (on BLU), and everyone else is on RED. Each boss has its own weapon, powers and voice lines. Most bosses' normal attack kills almost any class in one hit, so the trick for the other side is to stay out of reach and deal damage from a distance, as a team.

To keep things fair, **the boss's health grows with the number of players**. On our server, Saxton Hale has about **7,500** health against 6 opponents, **12,000** against 10 and **19,000** against 16.

## Who is Saxton Hale

Saxton Hale is the **owner of Mann Co.**, the company that sells TF2's weapons, hats and crates. He is a muscular, shirtless Australian who overdoes everything and solves every problem with his fists. For years he **only existed outside the game**: he first showed up in a magazine ad for "spy gadgets", spoofing the old Charles Atlas bodybuilding ads, in the Sniper vs. Spy Update, and then appeared in TF2's first official comic, in the WAR! Update.

Since he never showed up in a match, the community decided to put the head of Mann Co. in the fight himself. That is how the mode was born.

## How the mode started

- **VS Saxton Hale (VSH):** it began as a community-made mode, running on SourceMod servers, where one player controlled Saxton Hale against everyone else. It became one of TF2's most popular community modes.
- **Freak Fortress 2:** it came soon after, as an extension of VSH. The idea was simple: instead of only Saxton Hale, **any character can become a boss**, each with its own powers. Since then the community has created many bosses, from TF2's own characters to references to movies, cartoons and other games.
- **Official at Valve:** on **July 13, 2023**, in TF2's Summer Update, VS Saxton Hale **became an official game mode**, remade in VScript by community members (including LizardOfOz, who is also among the original credits of Saxton Hale in FF2), with the maps `vsh_tinyrock`, `vsh_distillery`, `vsh_skirmish` and `vsh_nucleus`.
- **Today:** FF2 is still alive and maintained by the community. Our server runs **Freak Fortress 2: Rewrite**, the most up-to-date version, with more bosses than the official mode.

## The bosses on our server

- **Saxton Hale:** the original. Super jump and a Rage that stuns everyone nearby, sentries included.
- **Vagineer:** a scary-faced Engineer; his Rage stuns and also gives him an ÜberCharge.
- **Horseless Headless Horsemann Jr.:** the "junior" version of TF2's Halloween boss. Instead of a super jump, he **teleports**.
- **Christian Brutal Sniper:** melee weapons only and lots of blood; his Rage gives him a bow.
- **The Easter Bunny:** the Easter Bunny, who "seems to want to give out candy". His Rage is called "happy easter, fools".
- **Seeman and Seeldier:** they always come **as a duo**, yelling "See?" and "See!". Seeman's Rage is an explosive dance; Seeldier's calls in clones.
- **Demopan:** the Demoman with a hat and a frying pan, the item-trading meme. His Rage is literally "trade spam".
- **Ninja Spy:** has 3 lives and 2 slow-motion attacks: look at an enemy and fire.
- **Gentle Spy:** teleports on Rage and gets an Ambassador.

## How to play

### If you are the boss

- **Super jump:** hold right-click to charge and, while looking up, release.
- **Weigh-down:** in midair, look down and crouch.
- **Anchor:** crouch on the ground so shots don't push you around.
- **Rage:** the meter fills as you **take damage**. When it's full, call for a Medic (`E` key) to unleash the boss's power.

### If you are against the boss

- **Stay away and stay together.** One hit from the boss usually kills, so ranged classes (Soldier, Demoman, Sniper, Heavy) keep the damage up, and the Medic keeps the team alive.
- **Watch the Rage.** When the boss's meter is almost full, back off: the stun hits everyone nearby.
- **Spy:** a **backstab** on the boss deals much more damage than usual, but it weakens if you repeat it back to back.
- **Engineer:** a **telefrag** (going through a teleporter and landing on top of the boss) deals **5,000** damage.
- **Use `!ff2weapons`** to see how each class's weapons change in this mode.

### Commands

- `!ff2`: the mode's main menu (`!hale` and `!vsh` also open it).
- `!ff2boss`: pick the boss you want to play when your turn comes.
- `!ff2queue`: see the queue for the next boss. Every round you play without being the boss earns you queue points.
- `!ff2music` and `!ff2voice`: turn the music and the bosses' voice lines on or off.
- `!ff2hud`: show or hide the round's damage board.

## Easter eggs and trivia

- **Vagineer's description is written backwards** in the boss file itself. Open `!ff2boss` and check it out.
- **`!pony`** also opens the FF2 menu. It's a hidden shortcut in the mode's code.
- **Demopan** was born on item-trading servers, where players shouted offers like "Stout Shako for 2 refined!". That line is in his description, and his Rage is called "trade spam".
- **The original Horseless Headless Horsemann** is the boss of the 2010 Halloween update. In FF2 he becomes "Jr." and swaps the super jump for a teleport.
- **Saxton Hale went more than ten years without appearing inside TF2** until he became an official mode in 2023. Before that, he only existed in comics, ads and, of course, on the community's VSH and FF2 servers.
