## 🎮 SKYNET GAMING BRASIL — TF2 Classic Maps

Welcome to the SKYNET GAMING community! We're a dedicated Team Fortress 2 server built for people who love classic TF2: full matches, traditional maps and a competitive atmosphere, always with respect.

## ⚙️ The server

- 40 slots and instant respawn
- 100 tick: more accurate shot registration than the default
- Hosted in São Paulo, with low ping across Brazil and South America
- Live ranking: every kill and every objective earns points
- Active admins and a transparent ban system

## 🗺️ Map rotation

Badwater, Dustbowl, Upward, Goldrush and 2Fort.

*Note: the server has 20+ maps installed. Maps outside the rotation may be added at the admins' discretion, on special occasions only.*
