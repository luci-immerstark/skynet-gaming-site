## 🎮 SKYNET GAMING BRASIL — TF2 Classic Maps

Welcome to the SKYNET GAMING community! We're a dedicated Team Fortress 2 server built for people who love classic TF2: full matches, traditional maps and a competitive atmosphere, always with respect.

## ⚙️ The server

- 40 slots and instant respawn
- 100 tick: more accurate shot registration than the default
- Hosted in São Paulo, with low ping across Brazil and South America
- Live ranking: every kill and every objective earns points
- Active admins and a transparent ban system

## 🗺️ Map rotation

Badwater, Dustbowl, Upward, Goldrush and 2Fort.

*Note: the server has 20+ maps installed. Maps outside the rotation may be added at the admins' discretion, on special occasions only.*

## 🚀 New: Dodgeball server

SKYNET GAMING now has a second server: **Dodgeball**, where everyone plays Pyro and deflects homing rockets. It has 8 slots, 100 tick, its own ranking and has been online since 10/06/2026. To join, type `connect 212.38.89.226:27025`. Want to understand the mode? Read [What Is TF2 Dodgeball](/en/blog/what-is-tf2-dodgeball/).

## 🏆 Grand opening event

**R$ 500 in prizes for the TOP 5 of the Classic Maps server ranking!**

- Start: Oct 30 at 8 PM (Brasília time, UTC-3)
- End: Nov 2 at 8 PM (Brasília time, UTC-3)

To take part, just play on the server. The top 5 players on the Classic Maps ranking when the event ends win the prizes. The Dodgeball ranking is separate and doesn't count for the event.

## 📊 Ranking

Type in the in-game chat: `rank`, `top10`, `session` or `place`.
Full ranking: [stats.skynet-tf2.com.br](https://stats.skynet-tf2.com.br)

## 📜 Rules

- Respect all players
- Cheats, exploits or any kind of cheating are forbidden
- No spam in chat or on the mic
- See something wrong? Use `!report` in chat or call an admin

Bans issued: [bans.skynet-tf2.com.br](https://bans.skynet-tf2.com.br)
